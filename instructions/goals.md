# Goals And Progress

These instructions govern real account goals under [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Analysis alone never counts toward account progress. Maintenance is not account setup, a monetary goal or trading approval.

## Budget And Target

The only user-supplied trading settings are a positive finite monetary trailing-loss **budget** and a positive finite monetary **target** in the verified account currency. Accept them together, for example "risk only 5 USD and make an extra 15 USD" in an actual trading-start request. Ask only for genuinely missing/ambiguous facts, such as amounts, currency or account identity, not percentages, allocated capital or routine symbol choices. Account facts come from actual MT5 MCP responses.

The target is additional cash-flow-adjusted account equity gain from the approved goal baseline, including floating P&L and estimated incremental closing costs. It is not turnover, deposits, starting capital, a desired ending balance, or a sum of closed-trade profits that ignores open losses. The allowance does not create a virtual subaccount. All manual and other-origin exposure/costs count.

In an explicit trading context, supplying the complete budget and target is actual start authorization under [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), including the final missing input after a trading-start request. It delegates the documented task-scoped MT5 operations, not unrelated/admin access, changed host permissions or a bypass of denied tools. Reuse the client's preconnected, execution-enabled MCP and verify the real account/schemas automatically. Do not ask again for MCP access, final named-account confirmation, individual orders or keep-going consent when scope is unambiguous. Technical access, drafts, examples, analysis, progress requests and maintenance are not trading intent. Record actual trusted request evidence, never a fabricated approval.

Prepare verified account/capability/universe evidence and save the complete money and mandate proposal through `draft` under the [helper contract](risk-management.md). A draft stores inputs, not authority by itself. Ace derives and discloses the named account, amounts, baseline/source times, broker symbol universe, snapshot freshness limit, entry timeframe, safety margin, any latch-only interval, bounded recovery and observed-only trailing definition. Include [Active Order Management](risk-management.md#active-order-management): Ace-only cost-aware breakeven/trailing stops, invalidation exits, pending-entry cancellation and protect-first winner additions within their original idea allocation. Routine management never adopts manual exposure; either monetary trigger invokes cancellation/closure of all account exposure including manual trades. These technical choices do not authorize new money, risk resets or scope expansion. Verify actual client continuation and disclose host limits, inactivity, missed peaks/breaches, gaps, slippage and delayed-close overshoots without another approval question.

For a direct explicit request, `activate` uses `approval_mode: "start_request"`; `approval_ref` identifies the actual trusted user request and `approved_at` is its actual timestamp. After preparing the proposal, capture and activate promptly without waiting for human review. Its baseline must be at or after the request, fresh at activation, and no later than the fresh current snapshot. Omitted `approval_mode` defaults to `reviewed` only for genuine historical reviewed receipts: preserve that frozen baseline, which may now be historical, and use fresh post-approval data. Never relabel an old receipt as a new request or rebase away intervening loss. Follow the exact [activation rules](risk-management.md#mandate-and-activation).

After successful activation, handle any activation-time latch/emergency immediately, then `session start` for genuinely authorized ongoing work, reuse an active session or `session resume` for verified interrupted-task recovery. Obtain a separate fresh first `cycle` before normal management/entries, even when none qualifies. Current analysis, instrument economics, costs, protection and affordability remain mandatory; persist each original plan/rationale at `prepare`, and spread risk across independent ideas with winner additions sharing the root entry's fixed allocation. Follow [Loop Sessions](loop.md): emergencies, existing-exposure management, eligible entries, readback/`resolve`, source-bound journal, consume revision/current control, actual wait and fresh read. The financial task remains unfinished until an observed budget/target latch and verified liquidation/reconciliation. No fills, unchanged orders, no affordable setup, a completed batch or saved report is completion. Actual client/broker permissions still apply; there is no target deadline, forced turnover, risk increase or promised return.

Reuse real existing authority without routine reapproval, but never expand an old bounded/reviewed mandate into a user-controlled ongoing session merely by upgrading state or instructions. Connection-only, analysis-only, dashboard-only, progress-only, maintenance and other bounded requests do not start sessions. Target pursuit remains capital-first and within the original mandate. A new goal, material scope change or risk reset needs explicit user instructions, not automatically generated prompts or separate repeated confirmation of an already explicit change.

## Equity-Based Progress

```text
E = current broker account equity, including floating valuation and booked costs
E0 = fixed verified equity at the authorized goal baseline
C_goal = net external capital flows since this goal's anchor
K = estimated incremental closing charges/slippage not already reflected in E

estimated_net_gain = E - C_goal - E0 - K
progress_pct = 100 * estimated_net_gain / target
remaining_amount = max(0, target - estimated_net_gain)
target_trigger = estimated_net_gain >= target
```

Keep `E0`, target and original history boundary fixed for that approved goal. A same-policy new goal gets its own goal/cash-flow anchor without resetting the risk policy's peak or loss latch. Do not clamp negative progress or progress above 100%; do not hide losses behind display rounding.

Obtain account equity/balance and complete relevant broker history from MCP. Classify external deposits/withdrawals/credits using actual broker semantics, paginate/deduplicate events and reconcile coverage with the account observation. Booked commission, spread valuation, swaps and fees already included in equity are not subtracted again. Unknown costs, missing conversion/history or a deposit not yet represented in the flow ledger make current progress unavailable, not zero or a verified crossing.

Existing floating profit at goal activation is already inside `E0`; it is not extra goal profit merely because the position later closes. The fixed interpretation is incremental whole-account mark-to-market gain from that baseline, not a new user carry-in accounting questionnaire. Preserve actual start marks/watermark and deferred-cost evidence; if that accounting cannot be verified, block additions rather than invent attribution.

The Python helper stores a full deduplicated external-flow ledger from an immutable history anchor and separate cash-flow anchors for policy and goal. Follow its exact JSON contract; do not populate extra undocumented fields or interpret a persisted flag as user authority. `status` retains historical timestamps and is not a current MT5 observation.

## Trigger And Close

On the first **actually observed and verified** `estimated_net_gain >= target`, latch the target stop. Do not infer an earlier floating-equity crossing from closed deals, invent a continuous peak, or backdate enforcement. An unobserved crossing may be missed while the AI is inactive.

Under the authorized named-account whole-account mandate:

1. Stop new entries and other risk increases immediately, including any instrument switch that would add exposure.
2. Cancel all pending entry orders, including manual/other-origin entries, using the actual documented MCP tool. Preserve necessary protective orders until their positions are safely closed.
3. Close all account positions including manual exposure using true position-close/reduce semantics. An opposite entry may hedge or reverse rather than close. Record intended actions before dispatch where the helper is available; an unavailable helper must not delay previously authorized identity-verified safety reductions.
4. Read back cancellations, partial/late fills, remaining orders, volume, stops and uncertain requests. Close remaining verified exposure under the same mandate, without blindly duplicating an uncertain close. Reconcile orphan orders only after their protection is no longer needed.
5. Verify flat positions, no pending orders, resolved intents and final costs. Record the actual final net gain and any shortfall separately from the trigger in the dashboard reporting workflow. A submitted request or accepted partial fill is not a flat account or the end of an active user-controlled session.

The stop stays latched after slippage, rejected/partial closes, equity recovery, profit falling below target, later cost corrections, new days and reconnects. Do not automatically trade to make up a shortfall. If a loss stop is also latched, loss handling takes precedence; neither latch hides the other.

Before a trigger, positions retain their original approved exits/ownership except for an explicitly instructed transition; the [active management rules](risk-management.md#active-order-management) do not retroactively expand authority. No-entry regimes or target/loss latches do not prevent identity-verified, separately still-authorized emergency reductions. A stopped/interrupted task blocks routine management, and no safety exception permits ignoring a user's revocation. After a whole-account trigger, its still-authorized cancellation/closure mandate overrides normal position holding periods, trailing decisions and add-on plans.

## Final Result And Resume

While exposure, costs or requests remain unresolved, report target-triggered/closure-pending, not a guaranteed achieved net profit. Once verified flat with final costs, actual incremental net gain is `final_equity - C_goal - E0` and shortfall is `max(0, target - final_net_gain)`. With a normal flat cash account, final balance equals equity; verify actual broker semantics rather than summing deal profits a second time.

For example, a target of `15` may trigger on estimated liquidation gain `15`, then close at actual net `14.7`. Record trigger observed, closure verified, final net `14.7`, shortfall `0.3`, and **financial stop latched**. An active user session continues observation without new risk; it is not stopped merely by that financial result. Do not falsely claim the final goal was met or clear the latch because the estimate changed. Later charges/corrections update the reported result, not the existing latch.

New-goal activation requires explicit user instructions for that goal, the applicable request-based or genuinely reviewed baseline, settled previous exposure/intents/costs, fresh current reconciliation and unchanged risk policy. Do not ask again when that change was already explicit and unambiguous. It preserves the risk peak and policy/flow anchor and archives the old financial loop; authorized ongoing work then obtains a fresh first `cycle` for the new loop. Unchanged financial `activate resume` retains the loop ID; legacy unbound state needs verified existing-authority binding, not routine new human approval or automatic task expansion. Explicit mandate `reauthorize` preserves identity, monetary baselines and latches; a changed authorized timeframe resets affected cadence/bar tracking only. A loss latch requires an explicitly instructed replacement policy/baseline; a target, reauthorization or task-control flag cannot release it. Financial activation or owner handoff never restarts a stopped task.

Financial `loop.status: "closed"` requires a monetary latch and **fresh** verified flat/no-pending exposure, settled intents and final costs. The user-controlled task is separate: an active `session` keeps `may_continue: true` through financial `active`, `closing` and `closed` states, including recoverable blockers. `journal`, `status` and `context` return cached canonical control, not fresh financial evidence. Neither `required_action: "none"`, a closed campaign, a journal acknowledgement nor reporting errors/absent fields end an active session. Session-absent legacy authority retains its original until-settlement completion; do not add ongoing monitoring without genuine authority.

After verified financial closure in an active user session, continue fresh account-only `check` observations at five minutes or sooner for a positive latch-check interval, with `wake_reason: "monitor"`. Deadlines follow accepted source/receipt evidence, not journal/control writes or browser refresh. Retain the financial loop ID, counters, `ended_at` and bar history; do not advance candles/cycles, open new risk or automatically reset the target/budget. Fresh late exposure returns that same loop to `closing` with latches intact for authorized reconciliation, not a new campaign.

Use the helper's `session` command only with activated state and the existing writer's `owner_id`, current `expected_revision`, `action`, opaque nonsecret `evidence_ref` of at most 200 characters, and nonblank `reason` of at most 500 characters. It accepts no `snapshot`. The exact [helper contract](risk-management.md) governs validation; these controls record actual task events, not new financial observations or fabricated authority.

| Session Action | Permitted Transition |
| --- | --- |
| `start` | From absent/stopped control, create a new task-control ID only under a genuinely authorized ongoing-session request. It does not reset financial state. |
| `resume` | Only from `interrupted`, retaining the same ID after actual existing-authority and host-recovery verification. |
| `stop` | Record explicit user end and clear planned deadlines; may also create a stopped legacy record without inventing a previous session start. |
| `interrupt` | Only from `active`, when an actual runtime blocker leaves no permitted continuation. Never an outage heartbeat or substitute for a user stop. |

`state.session` contains `id`, `status` (`active`, `stopped` or `interrupted`), `started_at`, `updated_at`, `request_ref`, `evidence_ref` and `reason`. Active means requested continuation, not proof of liveness. Control writes consume revisions but never observe an account, change financial anchors/latches/intents/`last_result`, refresh source age or create progress points. Consume canonical control returned by session writes and cached journal/status/context projections; historical `state.last_result` can predate stop/resume and cannot override it. Keep actual financial/action evidence separately. Dashboard status/reason/history may show these events, never their request/evidence references.

An explicit stop cannot be replaced by `interrupt` or `resume`; activation, owner handoff and new goals cannot undo it. Starting after a stop requires a genuinely new user request: previously consumed session/control references and authority held at earlier stops are rejected, and a novel string alone is not proof of new user intent. On user end, cancel client-scheduled continuations and record `session stop`; the helper control clears planned deadlines and blocks entries/routine management, but cannot cancel the client's scheduler itself. Original-intent recovery and separately still-authorized emergency reductions remain possible, not permission to ignore revoked authority or close positions solely because the task ended. If stopped after `prepare` but before dispatch, abandon the unused ordinary permission and retain/reconcile the original intent; do not invent nonexecution.

The AI client is the runtime. Prefer its documented persistent task/goal plus scheduled waits; otherwise use supported bounded interruptible waits in the same logical task. Register actual continuation before yielding when the host requires a turn boundary, not a promise of future reads. Never mark the native goal complete on financial closure while the user session is active or ask "continue?". Only explicit user end or unavoidable host interruption ends current task execution; neither completes or resets unfinished financial work. Honor denied tools and enforced limits, but an optional denied action does not end other permitted work. Host cancellation may prevent even recording a stop/interrupt event; never claim persistence when that write was unavailable.

No separate daemon, ad-hoc bridge or vendor configuration runs checks while the AI is inactive. Native waits and stored session flags are not continuous protection. Broker stop-losses remain essential but cannot guarantee the monetary cap or target. Report actual interruption, missing required capability or unresolved exposure honestly through the reporting workflow, with the next safe permitted step.

## Compact Trading Report

Routine reports belong in the source-bound journal and local dashboard, not a full per-cycle chat response. Use these fields as a concise dashboard/explanation checklist, not new JSON fields. Include actual values or `unavailable`, with factual decisions and outcomes rather than private chain-of-thought:

```text
Account / As Of: <login + server; demo/live; currency; connection; verified source times>
Budget / Risk: <budget; observed adjusted peak; drawdown; remaining money; stop floor; loss latch>
Target / Progress: <target; goal-start equity/time; estimated net gain; incremental close costs; progress/remaining>
Authority / Analysis: <actual authority or draft; authorized symbols; original strategy/root intent and allocation; current market read/invalidation, or no-entry reason>
Stop / Exposure: <target/loss latch; open/pending exposure; unresolved intent; closure pending or verified>
Session: <id; requested active/stopped/interrupted; actual control reason; not proof of liveness>
Loop: <id; financial active/closing/closed; execution/wait/blocker evidence; cycles/timeframe; last source; planned wake/reason; observation gap/max; cycle spacing separately>
Outcome / Next: <action/root intent/ticket and rationale; intended versus verified stop/cancel/close/add result or unknown remainder; final net/shortfall when verified; next permitted action>
```

During [loop-session reporting](loop.md), preserve the distinction between task control, financial lifecycle and execution evidence. Use `loop_health` source/receipt times, source age, actual source-to-source observation gaps and overdue-wake evidence; execution remains `unverified`. Raw loop `gap_seconds`/`max_gap_seconds` are cycle spacing, not account-observation gaps: intervening checks may observe risk. Legacy gap evidence stays unavailable, not inferred. No continuous monetary checks are proven between observations; neither a gap nor an overdue wake establishes an exact outage start/cause or crash.

Use chat only for the initial short started notice with verified dashboard URL, account and limits disclosure; a genuinely missing input; actual interruption or dashboard unavailability; user-requested progress; or user end. Do not claim a start, connected account or ready viewer before it is verified. When chat is needed, use concise relevant facts from this checklist, accurate source/time labels and the permitted next step, without routine confirmation or a conversational completion of still-active work. Put detailed mandate/analysis context in the source-bound dashboard reporting workflow. No-entry, quiet-market and ordinary closed-monitoring cycles do not require chat.

While authorized work remains executable, actually wait or take the due fresh read. If the native runtime requires a turn boundary, register real continuation before yielding; do not substitute a keep-going menu, "send a message to resume" or a future-monitoring promise. Transient MCP read failures use actual 5/10/20/30-second waits, capped at 30 seconds, and reset after verified recovery. Failed reads create no financial observation or journal heartbeat and never refresh source time. A non-observing `session interrupt` event is permitted only for actual runtime cessation when no permitted continuation remains, not a transient retry.

For management, keep `Outcome / Next` concise but distinguish proposed, broker-verified and partial/unknown outcomes. Record intended and verified old/new SL separately, the invalidation/expiry reason and actual remaining exposure for cancellation/closure, or why a valid order stayed unchanged. For a winner addition, identify the root entry intent and show its fixed allocation/use alongside account-wide headroom; breakeven is not zero give-back risk. Use existing rationale/journal text fields, not invented extra fields. Do not claim canceled, protected, filled or closed on submission alone.

Do not substitute the report-generation time for stale source timestamps, invent zero balances or assume a missing database is approved. Label the last actual snapshot/check; do not imply continuing monitoring, execution or reporting while inactive.

Before entries, obtain actual market evidence: authorized symbols, timeframes/source times, direction/range, volatility, spread relative to stops, session, invalidation, economics, costs/margin and total stressed risk against headroom. Save each original strategy's thesis, rules and rationale in [`action.context`](risk-management.md#decision-context) during `prepare`, outside exact MCP parameters, not only in a later journal or behind `analysis_ref`. The original intent ID and `action.risk` identify the idea/allocation. New routine management requires context linked to that root without replacing its plan; emergency metadata remains optional and cannot delay protection. Explicit `false` disables a rule, whereas missing legacy evidence stays unavailable. Report later outcomes/reassessments through source-bound linked journals. This is recoverable evidence and disclosure, not another approval stage, a strategy engine or permission to invent a signal.

## Dashboard Reporting

The [local dashboard](../README.md#local-dashboard) is the routine reporting channel for saved observations and explanations, not a new broker feed or an execution service. Keep it useful through the existing authorized workflow:

1. Perform the actual mandatory `check` or loop `cycle`, handle latches/emergencies first, then complete applicable management, MCP readback and `resolve` work. After financial closure in an active user session, use account-only monitoring checks without market/candle work or entries. A no-trade decision is a real outcome; do not repeat an observation command just to produce a report.
2. With an existing activated policy/goal, use the [exact `journal` contract](risk-management.md#journal-reports) against the final latest accepted `snapshot_id` and `as_of`. Record activity, a short summary, factual reason and next permitted step. Link one relevant root or related `intent_id` per report when available so `context` can recover its assessment after unrelated wait notes; add concise market/risk/invalidation text, symbols and nonsecret sources. Explain unchanged valid orders or why no entry qualified, not merely that a cycle happened. Reporting supplements the plan already saved at preparation.
3. Supply pending-order details only for exact tickets in that same snapshot and only from actual evidence. Distinguish known, unknown and explicitly unset values; do not infer Ace ownership or replace canonical protection/risk with display labels. Include manual/protective pending orders, not local intentions or invented native SL/TP rows. These remain read-only API evidence even though the Account Desk does not render a detailed broker-order list; its risk counts still use canonical order data.
4. Consume the journal's returned revision and cached canonical `session`, `loop`, `may_continue` and derived `loop_health`, retaining actual financial/action evidence separately. These pure projections never observe, move deadlines, change `last_result` or grant dispatch; older `last_result` cannot override current control. Never insert reporting between `prepare` and dispatch or ahead of emergency reductions, fabricate activity while inactive or use it as an outage heartbeat. Reassess rejected notes after state/source/goal changes, not blind rebinding. Reporting errors/absent fields leave explanations unavailable, not another approval gate, a task-stop signal or reason to delay protection. Factual helper and intent outcomes remain visible.
5. For active user sessions, including financially closed monitoring, actually wait to the earliest applicable wake or take the due fresh read under [Loop Sessions](loop.md). Use registered native continuation when a turn boundary is required, without routine chat reports. Journal/viewer success or failure cannot start/end the session, refresh source age or change its wake plan; dashboard unavailability is a reporting limitation, not grounds to delay safety or end otherwise permitted work.

The target overview uses the existing approved policy budget and goal, plus helper metrics (`estimated_goal_gain`, signed `progress_pct`, `remaining_target`, `remaining_close_costs`), not pending drafts, deposits as profit or balance divided by target. Its signed monetary scale runs from `-budget` through fixed zero to `+target`, with left/right section widths proportional to the approved budget/target. One solid gain fill starts at zero, extending right in green for positive `estimated_goal_gain` or left in red for negative gain, never both; zero has no fill and the gain label/marker moves with gain. Bound only fill/marker geometry to the endpoints, retaining exact money, signed percentage and overshoot text. The negative-budget endpoint is only a signed-gain visual reference, not real trailing-budget usage or a stop trigger. Keep actual trailing drawdown, headroom and latches separately in **Risk & state**. Show the approved baseline/time and both latches. Preserve loss priority and distinguish a target trigger, closure pending, verified `final_net` and `final_shortfall`; lower final profit never releases a stop.

Freshness comes from source `as_of` and authorized `max_snapshot_age_seconds`, not journal/control time, browser refresh or `last_cycle_at`. Display candle, latch-check and earliest wake separately so entry timing does not hide an earlier risk check. `loop_health` is a read-only derived view, not proof the agent is executing or MCP connected. Source/receipt gaps exclude exact replays, historical reviewed baselines, journal writes and session controls; future source times are errors. Show actual failed-read evidence separately from last-good metrics without inventing a journal heartbeat; missing/stale values are unavailable or labeled last-known with original timestamps, never zero. Missing state is unconfigured; drafts alone grant no authority. Corrupt, unsupported, busy or missing-after-load state is a read failure, never repair or approval.

The signed gain scale shows the latest saved metric, not interpolated returns or a continuous guard; a filled section is not proof of a latch or closure. The activity preview shows five newest events and its View all control reads older history through bounded, scoped pages without backfill. Older state and missing history/explanation text stay unavailable rather than reconstructed. Label prose as the agent's latest recorded explanation, never proof of execution, ownership or authority. Prepared/unknown intents stay visible and separate from canonical pending orders; settled requests can still be pending orders or terminal partial fills rather than filled trades or a flat account.

Current-campaign **Recorded Strategies** pages saved original plans/allocations, operation subtypes, linked action reasons and latest assessments, starting with 20 records. A strategy record is not proof of current ownership/exposure or a fresh signal. Completed entries can still be unfilled pending orders; missing legacy plans remain unavailable, without automatic backfill/adoption or symbol/ticket guesses. The dashboard allowlists this display data; raw `context`/`status` output, exact MCP parameters and private approval/receipt references must not enter its API. Keep the five-event activity preview, API-only pending-order details and absence of trading controls.

Task-control status/reasons/history are separate from those financial observations. Session events must not create target-progress points, change last-good financial results or expose request/evidence references or private action parameters. A stored active session means continuation was requested, never that an AI turn, scheduled wait or broker connection is currently executing.
