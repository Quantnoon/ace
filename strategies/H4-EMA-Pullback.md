# H4-EMA-Pullback

Kind: member strategy (AI-generated hypothesis)
Version: 1.0
Provenance: generated by Ace during the 2026-11-03 trading-onboarding session for account
41180154 (Deriv-Demo). Not user-supplied. No prior source or result exists for this rule set.

## Rationale

A trend-continuation complement to H4-Donchian-Breakout. Instead of buying strength at a new
extreme, it waits for an established trend to pull back into the fast moving average and buys
the first resumption bar. On H4 the stop distance is large enough that the recorded spread plus
slippage is a small fraction of risk, which was the main defect of the archived M15 members.

## Scope

- Primary entry timeframe: H4. Requires at least 12 calendar months of evaluation.
- Instruments: broker-mapped FX majors and metals with verified contract economics.
- Symmetric long/short; no news or session filter.

## Indicators (fixed)

- EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200) on H4 close.
- ATR(14), Wilder smoothing, on H4 bars.

## Entry

- Long: EMA(20) > EMA(50) > EMA(200); the bar's low touches or undercuts EMA(20); the bar
  closes above EMA(20); the bar is bullish (close > open).
- Short: EMA(20) < EMA(50) < EMA(200); the bar's high touches or exceeds EMA(20); the bar
  closes below EMA(20); the bar is bearish (close < open).
- Fill at the next H4 bar open plus modeled spread/slippage.

## Protective exits

- Initial stop: lowest low of the last 3 bars minus 0.5 x ATR(14) for longs; mirror for shorts.
- Take profit: 2.0 x the entry-to-stop distance.
- Trend-break exit: close beyond EMA(50) against the position.
- Hard time exit: 60 H4 bars.
- If a bar contains both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed first.

## Sizing and portfolio

Identical to the bundle allocator: 0.33% of current modeled equity per trade, volume rounded
down to the broker step, minimum lot skipped rather than enlarged, at most 3 concurrent
positions in the shared-capital portfolio.

## Costs

Bar-recorded spread, 5 points per side slippage on 5-digit FX and 10 points on XAUUSD,
commission 0.0 modeled, verified swap rates applied with 3x Wednesday financing.

## Observation and monetary stops

The whole-account observed-only trailing USD budget and equity target apply unchanged. No
continuous guard exists.

## Known limitations

- Pullback entries can be run over by a fast trend reversal; the 2R target caps upside while
  the trend filter can delay entries.
- Recorded historical bar spread is not a verified historical fill distribution.
