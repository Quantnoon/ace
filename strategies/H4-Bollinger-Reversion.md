# H4-Bollinger-Reversion

Kind: member strategy (AI-generated hypothesis)
Version: 1.0
Provenance: generated by Ace during the 2026-11-03 trading-onboarding session for account
41180154 (Deriv-Demo). Not user-supplied. No prior source or result exists for this rule set.

## Rationale

The range-regime member of the bundle. The archived M15 range member lost most on cost drag and
on taking mean-reversion trades during trending conditions. This version only trades when a
verified low-ADX condition says the market is ranging, uses a wider H4 stop, and exits at the
mean rather than at a fixed target.

## Scope

- Primary entry timeframe: H4. Requires at least 12 calendar months of evaluation.
- Instruments: broker-mapped FX majors and metals with verified contract economics.
- Symmetric long/short; no news or session filter.

## Indicators (fixed)

- Bollinger Bands(20, 2.0) on H4 close, population standard deviation.
- ADX(14), Wilder smoothing, on H4 bars.
- ATR(14), Wilder smoothing, on H4 bars.

## Entry

- Regime gate: ADX(14) strictly below 20.0 on the signal bar. No entry otherwise.
- Long: completed H4 close strictly below the lower band.
- Short: completed H4 close strictly above the upper band.
- Fill at the next H4 bar open plus modeled spread/slippage.

## Protective exits

- Initial stop: entry minus 1.5 x ATR(14) for longs; mirror for shorts.
- Target: the middle band (SMA 20) reached intrabar.
- Hard time exit: 30 H4 bars.
- If a bar contains both the stop and the middle band, the stop is assumed first.

## Sizing and portfolio

0.33% of current modeled equity per trade, volume rounded down to the broker step, minimum lot
skipped rather than enlarged, at most 3 concurrent positions in the shared-capital portfolio.

## Costs

Bar-recorded spread, 5 points per side slippage on 5-digit FX and 10 points on XAUUSD,
commission 0.0 modeled, verified swap rates applied with 3x Wednesday financing.

## Observation and monetary stops

The whole-account observed-only trailing USD budget and equity target apply unchanged. No
continuous guard exists.

## Known limitations

- Low-ADX is a lagging regime label; a range can break out immediately after entry.
- Mean-reversion systems carry tail risk that a fixed ATR stop does not fully bound across gaps.
- Recorded historical bar spread is not a verified historical fill distribution.
