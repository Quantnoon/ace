## Regime classifier (fixed, observable, no hindsight)

Evaluated on each completed H4 bar, using only that bar and earlier bars:

- ADX(14), Wilder smoothing, on H4.
- Efficiency ratio over 20 bars = |close[i] - close[i-20]| / sum(|close[j] - close[j-1]|).

| Condition | Label |
| --- | --- |
| ADX > 25.0 and ER > 0.30 | `trend` |
| ADX < 20.0 | `range` |
| otherwise | `none` (no new entry) |

Thresholds are fixed before execution. There is no discretionary override and no re-labelling
of past bars.

## Deterministic selector and allocation

For each symbol, on each completed H4 bar, in this order:

1. `trend` -> evaluate H4-Donchian-Breakout. If it signals, that is the entry. If not,
   evaluate H4-EMA-Pullback and take its signal if present.
2. `range` -> evaluate H4-Bollinger-Reversion only.
3. `none` -> no new entry.

Allocation and concurrency:

- One position per symbol. A symbol with an open position or a pending fill is not re-signalled.
- At most 3 concurrent positions in the shared-capital portfolio.
- Competing simultaneous signals are resolved by the frozen symbol priority order:
  EURUSD, then GBPUSD, then XAUUSD.
- Risk 0.33% of current modeled equity per trade to the protective stop.

## Transitions

- A regime change governs future eligibility only. It never closes, reverses or reassigns an
  existing position and never widens a stop.
- An open position keeps its originating member's exit rules until that member exits it, or
  until the whole-account monetary stop applies.
- No fallback member is invented if a member's rules produce no signal.

## Monetary stops

The account mandate's observed-only trailing USD budget and equity-based target apply to the
whole account, including any manual exposure. At either latch, new risk stops, all pending
entries are cancelled and all positions are closed. Loss handling takes precedence. There is no
independent continuous guard.

## Limitations

- The regime classifier is lagging and threshold-sensitive; `none` periods are reported, not
  hidden.
- Member edges are unproven; this is an unreviewed hypothesis set, not a validated system.
- The budget is 1.674% of modeled equity, so the observed-only stop is likely to bind before
  any strategy's natural drawdown completes.
