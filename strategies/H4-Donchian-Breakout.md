# H4-Donchian-Breakout

Kind: member strategy (AI-generated hypothesis)
Version: 1.0
Provenance: generated by Ace during the 2026-11-03 trading-onboarding session for account
41180154 (Deriv-Demo). Not user-supplied. No prior source or result exists for this rule set.

## Rationale

The three archived M15 FX members (TrendPullback-EMA-M15, RangeReversion-Bollinger-M15,
Adaptive-Regime-FX-M15) all failed their pre-declared suitability criteria on
2026-08-01..2026-11-03 with profit factors of 0.70-0.93. Their recorded round-trip cost
(bar-recorded spread plus 0.5 pip slippage per side) was roughly 20% of a typical M15 stop
distance, so the failure is consistent with structural cost drag on a sub-H4 entry timeframe.

This hypothesis moves the entry timeframe to H4 and uses wide, volatility-scaled stops, so the
same absolute spread is a much smaller fraction of each unit of risk. It is a classic
trend-following breakout: participate when price leaves a long consolidation in the direction
of the dominant trend, cut losers at a fixed ATR distance, and let the trailing channel define
the exit.

## Scope

- Primary entry timeframe: H4. H4 entry requires at least 12 calendar months of evaluation.
- Instruments: broker-mapped FX majors and metals with verified contract economics.
- No news filter, no session filter, symmetric long/short.

## Indicators (fixed)

- Donchian channel, 55 bars, computed on completed bars only and excluding the signal bar.
- Donchian exit channel, 20 bars, same exclusion rule.
- EMA(200) on H4 close.
- ATR(14), Wilder smoothing, on H4 bars.

## Entry

- Long: completed H4 close is strictly above the 55-bar Donchian upper channel AND strictly
  above EMA(200).
- Short: completed H4 close is strictly below the 55-bar Donchian lower channel AND strictly
  below EMA(200).
- Fill at the next H4 bar open (ask for longs, bid for shorts) plus modeled slippage.
- One position per symbol; no pyramiding; no re-entry on the same bar.

## Protective exits

- Initial stop: entry minus 2.0 x ATR(14) for longs, entry plus 2.0 x ATR(14) for shorts.
  The stop is sent as a broker-side stop with the entry.
- Trailing exit: close beyond the opposite 20-bar Donchian channel.
- Hard time exit: 180 H4 bars.
- No take-profit; the channel is the only profit exit.
- If a bar contains both the stop and the channel exit, the stop is assumed first.

## Sizing and portfolio

- Risk 0.33% of current modeled equity per trade to the protective stop (about 19.71 USD at the
  verified 5973.27 USD modeled equity).
- Volume = floor(risk / (stop distance x contract size) / volume step) x volume step, never
  rounded up; a minimum lot that would exceed the risk target is skipped, not enlarged.
- At most 3 concurrent positions across the shared-capital portfolio, allocated in the frozen
  symbol priority order.

## Costs

- Bar-recorded spread applied against the trade (buy at ask, sell at bid).
- Modeled slippage: 5 points per side on 5-digit FX, 10 points per side on XAUUSD.
- Commission modeled as 0.0 (Deriv forex is spread-only on the verified account; not verified
  as a historical fact).
- Financing modeled from the verified per-lot swap rates, 3x on Wednesday.

## Observation and monetary stops

The whole-account observed-only trailing USD budget and equity target from the account mandate
apply unchanged. There is no continuous guard; a missed observation can miss a peak or breach.

## Known limitations

- Breakout systems have low win rates and long losing streaks; a 100 USD trailing budget on a
  5973.27 USD account is only 1.674% of equity and will stop the account well before a typical
  breakout equity drawdown completes.
- Recorded historical bar spread is not a verified historical fill distribution.
- No news, liquidity or holiday filter.
